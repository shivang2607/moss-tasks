# Cheapest 16 GB VPS in India: price comparison

Scope: VPS plans with 16 GB RAM and a physical India server location. Compare advertised INR pricing and disclose advance-payment, renewal, tax and availability caveats. No account creation or purchase.

## Recommendation

Host IT Smart VPS - SM 3: 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, 200 GB SSD, 16 TB bandwidth, Linux, self-managed. The official page describes its VPS hardware as located in a Tier 4 Indian data center. It advertises INR 730/month with INR 17,520 paid upfront for 24 months, plus six extra months. The configuration page confirmed: "We've added 6 extra months to your 2-year VPS plan" and "18% GST will be applicable*". This makes the initial 30-month effective rate INR 584/month before GST (17,520 / 30), or INR 689.12/month including GST. Calculated upfront total including GST: INR 20,673.60 (17,520 x 1.18). Listed renewal: INR 1,630/month before GST. This is an introductory long-term offer, not a monthly-payment price. Optional control panels and backups cost extra; the observed summary total was the base INR 17,520 with no backup selected.

Source: https://www.hostitsmart.com/servers/vps-hosting

Configuration evidence: https://www.hostitsmart.com/cart/newconfig?id=0 (session-specific cart, reached by selecting VPS - SM 3; no purchase or checkout submission).

This is the cheapest advertised India-hosted 16 GB offer found among the plans checked, not a guarantee of the lowest price across every provider. Server performance, support quality and live provisioning were not tested. The provider states India hosting, but the exact city for this offer was not established. Do not confuse the effective monthly cost with monthly billing.
